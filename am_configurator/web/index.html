OpenKeeb is an independent, open-source community project for configuring compatible keyboards locally.

Angry Miao, Vial, and VIA describe compatible hardware or firmware ecosystems; OpenKeeb is not affiliated with or endorsed by their vendors or maintainers.

Portable profile

OpenKeeb profile

An OpenKeeb profile is the portable cross-keyboard format. Open one as a document, export the current keyboard snapshot, or transfer compatible keymap data onto this target. The review shows what carried over, what adapted, and what still needs a decision. Nothing touches a keyboard until Write.
