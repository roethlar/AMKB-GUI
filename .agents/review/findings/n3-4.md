## Evidence

am_configurator/hid_transport.py:156 — classification searches OSError text for permission or busy words, but the locked hidapi 0.15 binding reports every open_path failure as the generic 'open failed'.

## Predicted observable failure

Permission-denied and busy devices are reported as detached, so Linux users never see the udev remedy and users cannot distinguish contention from absence.

## What

Every open failure is therefore reported as 'The keyboard is no longer
attached.' On Linux the udev remedy — the entire point of N3c — is unreachable.

## Approach

Not started. The reviewer's suggestion: Use errno-bearing platform preflight where available, re-enumeration or backend-specific diagnostics elsewhere, and return a truthful generic unavailable error when the cause cannot be distinguished.
