## Evidence

am_configurator/hid_transport.py:176 - product_id is AM Neon 80 during shallow discovery but NEON80 after deep identification; server.py:2098 requires the confirmation to equal the deep value exactly.

## Predicted observable failure

The browser asks the user to confirm AM Neon 80 and submits that value, after which the server rejects every write with Confirmation must exactly match NEON80.

## What

See the pass summary in `.agents/review/index.md`: the protocol modules are
sound in isolation and the seams between them and the existing application were
not built. This finding is one of those seams.

## Approach

Shallow discovery and deep identification now expose one canonical product ID,
while the firmware-authored USB product string remains a separate display name.
An unrecognized board receives no product ID.

## Files changed

`am_configurator/hid_transport.py` and `tests/test_hid_transport.py`.

## Guard proof

The canonical-ID, display-name, and unrecognized-board guards fail if discovery
and identification diverge. The repair was red-proven as part of `48398c9`.
