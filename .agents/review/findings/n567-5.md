## Evidence

am_configurator/vial_keymap.py:144 - to_qmk rejects every natural page except 0x07, including the application's None code #00000000 and macro tokens #00951500 through #0095150F.

## Predicted observable failure

Clearing a key or assigning any UI-created macro makes the entire Neon keymap fail preflight, so core keymap and macro parity is unavailable.

## What

See the pass summary in `.agents/review/index.md`: the protocol modules are
sound in isolation and the seams between them and the existing application were
not built. This finding is one of those seams.

## Approach

The application's empty-key spelling maps uniquely to `KC_NO`, and each macro
token maps to its Vial macro keycode. Duplicate spelling and out-of-range macro
slots are rejected rather than coerced.

## Files changed

`am_configurator/vial_keymap.py` and `tests/test_vial_keymap.py`.

## Guard proof

The empty-key, unique-spelling, every-macro-slot, out-of-range, and realistic
keymap guards all fail when their mappings are removed. Both mappings were
red-proven before restoration.
